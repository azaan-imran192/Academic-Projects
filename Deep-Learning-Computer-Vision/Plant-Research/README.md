# Plant Research

Two related but distinct academic components are grouped here without treating their implementations or results as interchangeable.

| Component | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Disease Classification](Disease-Classification/) | InceptionV3 + Transformer experiments on three leaf-image datasets | Notebooks, research report, license, and model references imported |
| [Phenotyping Dashboard](Phenotyping-Dashboard/) | Django APIs, image segmentation integration, and plant/leaf trait calculations | Sanitized backend imported; commercial-template frontend held |

The classification accuracies belong to the research experiments. They are not dashboard validation metrics. Research images, database contents, and model binaries are not bundled. See each component for attribution, setup, limitations, and verification.
